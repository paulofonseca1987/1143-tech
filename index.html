Cap. I

What this is

1143. The year Portugal became a kingdom, at the Treaty of Zamora.1143.tech is a showcase of modern Portuguese military technology: the companies, engineers and labs building defense tech in Portugal today, in one place, so the people who need them can find them.

For decades Europe let someone else worry about its defense. That time is over. The continent has to build, buy and field its own capability again, and it has to do it quickly.

Portugal looks after one of the largest maritime areas in Europe, from the coast to the Azores.Portugal can help more than most people think. Portuguese teams are shipping drones, autonomous vessels, sensors, satellites and secure networks right now. Most of that work stays invisible outside Portugal.

We put it in front of the people who need it: armed forces and procurement teams looking for capability, investors looking for the next defense company, and engineers looking for work that matters.

Cap. III

Of the line

Forte, from the Latin fortis: brave. For belicosa, see the note under the title.The line comes from Os Lusíadas, Camões' epic of 1572, the poem every Portuguese kid reads in school. It's Vasco da Gama's answer to a ruler who wanted to know who these strangers were.

Four and a half centuries later it's the motto of Base Aérea n.º 8 in Ovar, where Portuguese F-16s flew. We took it as ours, because it says what we believe: Portugal builds again, and Europe stands up for itself again.
